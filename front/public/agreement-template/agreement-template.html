Appendix 1 – Hostel Rules





  1. Behavior



    1. Accommodation at the Hostel is provided only to female students and staff of NSBM, the University of Colombo, the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and such other educational institutions or categories of Resident as may be accepted by the Proprietor.



    1. The Resident shall not let any outsider visit the Hostel or Hostel Room without obtaining prior consent from the Hostel Warden.



    1. The Residents are expected to behave in an acceptable manner anywhere within the Hostel and Hostel Rooms.



    1. The noise level, and the volume of music must be always kept low.



    1. Pranks and ragging in any form are strictly prohibited.



    1. The Resident must return to the Hostel by 8:00 pm, daily for their safety. Residents who need to stay outside the Hostel premises after 8:00 pm, must inform the Hostel Warden and / or the Proprietor. This communication shall be received from a parent/guardian of the Resident to the Hostel Warden.



    1. The Resident must not leave the Hostel premises after 8 pm unless permission is granted and informed to the Hostel Warden or Proprietor by a parent/guardian of the Resident for the specific day.



    1. If the Resident has any known medical conditions, it must be informed to the Hostel Warden and should be mentioned in the Registration form submitted by the Resident.



  1. Cleanliness of the Hostel and Hostel Rooms



    1. The Residents are always responsible for the keeping, cleanliness and tidiness of their rooms, including the common kitchen. The Resident must wash all the dishes once she finishes cooking in the kitchen.



    1. All fans, lights & electrical appliances must be switched off when not in use or when leaving the room. (Do not use fans to dry wet clothes).



    1. The Resident shall not use any cooking and heating appliances (Hot plates, Infrared cookers, Induction cookers and Heaters) inside the Hostel Rooms and shall be used only in common kitchens. However, the Resident is allowed to use an electric kettle inside the Hostel Room subject to obtaining prior approval from the Hostel Warden.



    1. Use of water heaters is strictly prohibited within the Hostel and Hostel Rooms. A serious or repeated breach of this rule may result in termination of the Resident's accommodation in accordance with Clause 7.5 of the Hostel Accommodation Agreement. Any loss or damage caused by such breach may be recovered from the Resident in accordance with the Agreement.



    1. Where Allocated Accommodation provided on a sharing basis is not fully occupied (2 or 3 persons based on capacity), the Resident shall not use any unallocated beds, tables, cupboards or racks. The Resident shall ensure that the room is clean and tidy at all times and when displaying to new residents.



    1. The Residents should dispose of garbage, including waste food, polythene, paper and sanitary napkins into the respective bins placed on the Hostel premises. (Please contact the Hostel Warden regarding any clarification on the above). Should there be a block of the drainage pipes due to the negligent disposal of sanitary napkins by Resident(s), the relevant Resident(s) shall reimburse reasonable repair cost plus the applicable administration fee under the Agreement.



    1. The Resident is responsible for the proper care of the items/equipment and facilities provided inside the Hostel Room and common kitchen. In case of any loss/damage to any Hostel Room caused by the Resident, the Proprietor has the right to claim the rectification cost of the loss/damage from the Resident. The Resident shall reimburse reasonable repair cost plus the applicable administration fee under the Agreement to rectify the damage.



    1. Where loss or damage occurs to a shared Hostel Room, common area or shared item, the Proprietor may recover the reasonable rectification cost from the Resident or Residents whose responsibility for such loss or damage is reasonably established. Where damage is jointly caused by more than one Resident, the reasonable cost may be apportioned between the responsible Residents.



  1. Security of Personal Items



    1. The Residents are fully responsible for all of their personal belongings and valuable items such as mobile phones, laptops, watches, money, jewellery etc. The Residents shall ensure that the doors and windows of the Allocated Accommodation are properly closed. The Proprietor, the Proprietor’s Representative, and the Hostel Warden may provide reasonable assistance to the Resident to find any lost item. However, the Proprietor, the Proprietor’s Representative, the Hostel Warden, or any other person working in the Hostel premises cannot be held responsible by the Resident for the loss of personal items. Accordingly, the risk of loss, theft or damage to the Resident’s personal belongings shall be borne by the Resident and not by the Proprietor, except to the extent that such liability cannot lawfully be excluded.



    1. Where the Proprietor has reasonable grounds to believe that a Resident has been involved in theft or other serious illegal conduct, the Proprietor may terminate the Resident's accommodation in accordance with Clause 7.5 and may report the matter to the relevant authorities where appropriate. Any Security Deposit shall be dealt with in accordance with Clause 4.0 of the Agreement and shall not be automatically forfeited solely by reason of termination.



    1. If the Resident damages or misplaces a smart card or key, the Resident shall pay the following replacement charge:



  1. Smart Card – 3,000 rupees;

  2. Key of the Toilet Door – 2,000 rupees;

  3. Key of the Cupboard Door – 2,000 rupees.



    1. Visitors, including parents, may enter the Hostel or Hostel Room only with the prior permission of the Hostel Warden.



    1. No visitors are allowed to stay overnight without any permission from the Hostel Warden.
